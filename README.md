Caveman Runner
Survival isn't just a goal—it's a full-time job.

Step back into the Stone Age in Caveman Runner, an adrenaline-fueled 2D side-scrolling survival game. You play as a primitive hero who has wandered a bit too far into the nesting grounds of the Jurassic period’s deadliest residents.

With a hungry T-Rex hot on your heels and a path blocked by prehistoric perils, your only options are to run fast, jump high, and slide low. How many kilometers can you survive before you’re extinct?

 Key Features
Infinite Prehistoric Action: The further you run, the faster it gets. Test your reflexes as the world speeds up and the obstacles multiply.

A Deadly Bestiary: Dodge venomous snakes, avoid diving birds, outrun predatory raptors, and leap over massive rolling stones.

Fluid Movement: Master the mechanics of jumping and sliding to navigate a dynamic hitbox system.

Immersive Soundscape: Featuring a cinematic "Hall of Fame" soundtrack and authentic (and terrifying) dinosaur roars.

Boss-Level Threat: Keep an eye on the T-Rex lurking in the background—he’s just waiting for you to trip.

Custom UI: Includes a "Kilometer" tracker, a pause system for quick breaks, and unique "Death Screens" tailored to the creature that caught you.

 How to Play
The controls are simple, but the timing is everything:

UP ARROW: Jump over stones and snakes.

DOWN ARROW: Slide under low-flying birds or "fast-fall" back to the ground.

START/PAUSE: Use the on-screen buttons to manage your session.

 Built With
Language: Python

Library: Pygame

Art: High-quality 2D sprites and parallax-ready backgrounds.
